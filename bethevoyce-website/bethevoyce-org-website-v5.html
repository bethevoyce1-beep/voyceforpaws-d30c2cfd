Our Technology

Voyce for Paws — the app

See a stray or injured animal? Snap a photo and our AI reads the animal as it is — building a rescue card, finding the location, and alerting nearby helpers.

  • Take or upload a photo
  • AI builds a rescue profile & health assessment
  • Location detected with a map & directions
  • Shared to rescuers & fosters nearby

Why We Exist

Connection is the missing link.

Every day, people want to help. Every day, animals need help. Every day, families need support. The challenge isn’t compassion — it’s connection.

Be The Voyce exists to bring communities together through innovative programs, technology, and partnerships that make helping easier and more effective.
