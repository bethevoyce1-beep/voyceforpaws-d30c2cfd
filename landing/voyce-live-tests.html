Why "at-risk only"

This zone is for animals on the kill list — nothing else.

Voyce supports five missions: Emergency (injured strays), At-Risk (shelter euthanasia — this page), Reunite (lost & found), Prevention (TNR, spay/neuter), and Wildlife (licensed rehabbers).

The "live testing" zone is narrowly focused on shelter kill lists because that's the most time-sensitive, most concrete, most provable use case. Each shelter publishes a daily list of animals about to be euthanized as a public PDF. Voyce takes that list, surfaces the animals as shareable cards, and alerts the rescue network — before the deadline.
