Cookie Policy

Effective Date: April 20, 2026  ·  Be The Voyce Animal Rescue Network

The short version: We use a small number of cookies to keep the site working and to understand how it's used. We don't use cookies to track you for advertising, build profiles about you, or sell your data — ever.

1.What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device when you visit them. They help websites remember things like whether you're logged in, what you've clicked, or which pages you've seen. Most cookies are harmless — some are essential to making the site work, and others help us understand how visitors use our platform.

2.The Cookies We Use

🔒 Essential Cookies

These keep the platform working. They remember if you're logged in, if you've agreed to our Terms, and keep forms from resetting if the page reloads. Without these, basic features like sign-in and the contact form wouldn't work.

📊 Performance Cookies

These help us understand how visitors use Voyce for Paws — which pages get viewed, how long people spend on them, whether forms get completed. We use this to fix issues and improve the experience. All data is aggregated and anonymous — we never link it to individual users.

🗺️ Third-Party Cookies (Limited)

A few features load from other services, and those services may set their own cookies:

  • Leaflet & CartoDB — for the interactive coverage map (map tiles only, no personal tracking)
  • Formspree — for processing your email signups, contact messages, and registration (essential to forms)
  • unpkg / CDNs — to load fonts and JavaScript libraries (delivery only)

These third parties have their own cookie and privacy policies. None of them are used for advertising on our site.

3.What We Don't Use

We never use:

  • Advertising cookies or ad networks
  • Cross-site tracking for marketing
  • Data brokers or resellers
  • Social media retargeting pixels
  • Behavioral profiling beyond basic site analytics

4.Your Choices

You're in control. You can manage or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings. Here's how in common browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Heads up: If you disable essential cookies, some parts of Voyce for Paws (like logging in or submitting the contact form) may not work properly.

5.Do Not Track

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. Because there's no agreed-upon standard for responding to this signal, we treat all visitors the same — minimal, non-advertising cookies only. We believe that's the right default for everyone, regardless of a Do Not Track setting.

6.Changes to This Policy

If we change how we use cookies, we'll update this page and the "Effective Date" at the top. For significant changes, we'll also notify registered users via email or in-app message.
