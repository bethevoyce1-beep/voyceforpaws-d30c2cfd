6.AI Triage Estimates, Health Assessments & Limitations

6.1 AI-Generated Content. The Voyce for Paws app uses artificial intelligence (“AI”) to analyze photographs of animals and generate a preliminary assessment called an “AI Triage Estimate.” This estimate may include the AI’s assessment of the animal’s breed, approximate age, weight, coat condition, visible injuries or health indicators, and an estimated urgency level. All AI-generated content is clearly labeled as “AI Assessment” and is preliminary in nature.

6.2 Not Veterinary Advice. AI Triage Estimates are NOT veterinary examinations, diagnoses, prescriptions, or treatment plans. They are automated visual assessments that may be incomplete, inaccurate, or incorrect. No veterinarian–client–patient relationship (VCPR) is created or implied by any AI-generated content on the platform. The AI cannot detect internal conditions, pain levels, diseases, parasites, or any condition not visible in a photograph.

6.3 Reporter-Submitted Observations. Users may manually add observations to supplement the AI Triage Estimate (e.g., behavioral observations, circumstances of discovery, visible symptoms the AI may have missed). These notes are labeled “Reporter Observation” and represent the subjective observations of untrained individuals. Be the Voyce, Inc. does not verify, endorse, or guarantee the accuracy of reporter-submitted information.

6.4 Veterinary Confirmation. Licensed veterinarians and credentialed veterinary technicians on the Voyce network may review, edit, correct, and confirm an AI Triage Estimate. Upon confirmation, the card becomes a “Vet-Confirmed Health Assessment” bearing the confirming professional’s name and credentials. Even Vet-Confirmed Health Assessments are informational summaries based on available data and do not replace a full in-person veterinary examination.

6.5 Source Tagging. All information displayed on cards is tagged with its source: “AI Assessment,” “Reporter Observation,” or “Vet Confirmed.” Users acknowledge that source tags indicate the origin and reliability level of each piece of information and agree to consider these tags when making decisions.
